# Field Technician Copilot: worked example

> Run on synthetic data with `llm-agent` engine, seed 44957, knobs {}, in 1.9 s on Python 3.13.18. Every number below is read from `starter/results.json`, which `make test` regenerates and checks. Synthetic results are easier than production: they show the mechanics and the traps, not what you will measure on your data.

## 1. Frame the case

**Objective.** Answer technician questions on site about equipment, procedures and safety steps from controlled manuals and past work orders, in the technician's language, without altering any record.

**Constraints that shape the design.** Safety steps shown verbatim from controlled documents; The assistant cannot issue a switching or isolation instruction; Works with intermittent connectivity

**Label in this use case.** For "Field Technician Copilot": Answer technician questions on site about equipment, procedures and safety steps from controlled manuals and past work orders, in the technician's language, without altering any record. Define the label exactly as in milestone M1: what counts as a positive or a correct outcome, and when it becomes known. Design around this trap first: Paraphrased safety steps.

## 2. Choose the signals

Your strongest signal is **Equipment identification** (asset type and model). Its rule: From the work order. In the starter, the closest executable idea is **typed read-only tools**: narrow queries for context and evidence, with no free-form shell or query language. Map your columns in `starter/` before you change anything else; the full list of signals and engine features is in `starter/usecase.json`.

## 3. Meet the first trap

Records can carry planted instructions. The naive agent followed them in 88.9% of injected cases and took 6 unsafe actions; it also decided cases whose deciding evidence was unavailable.

## 4. Baseline against the stronger design

The guarded agent treats tool output as data, verifies every claim against a cited line and escalates when evidence is missing: injection success 0.0%, unsafe actions 0, accuracy on the cases it decides 100.0%, escalating 6.0%.

| Result | Value |
|---|---|
| Injection success, naive | 88.9% |
| Injection success, guarded | 0.0% |
| Unsafe actions, naive versus guarded | 6 vs 0 |
| Accuracy on auto-decided cases, guarded | 100.0% |
| Escalation rate, guarded | 6.0% |

## 5. An edge case from the blueprint

**No connectivity.** the technician is underground. Planned response: Cached manuals and sync. Write the test that replays this case through your pipeline before you tune anything.

## 6. What to expect on your own data

The planner is scripted so the harness is deterministic. Keep the planted-injection tests, the step budget and the verifier when you put a model behind the same typed tools.

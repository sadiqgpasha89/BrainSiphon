- **Baseline model:** Letter templates filled by hand by caseworkers, because it is the current process.
- **Production model:** A drafting assistant constrained to recorded reasons with template-based appeal sections, plain language checks, translation and a signature step.
- **Intended use:** Help caseworkers write clear decision letters from the recorded reasons and legal basis and tell people how to appeal, without changing the decision or adding reasons.

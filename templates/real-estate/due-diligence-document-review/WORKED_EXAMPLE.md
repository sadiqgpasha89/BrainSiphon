# Real Estate Due-Diligence Document Review: worked example

> Run on synthetic data with `llm-agent` engine, seed 32037, knobs {}, in 0.6 s on Python 3.13.13. Every number below is read from `starter/results.json`, which `make test` regenerates and checks. Synthetic results are easier than production: they show the mechanics and the traps, not what you will measure on your data.

## 1. Frame the case

**Objective.** Review data-room documents (title, leases, surveys, environmental, contracts) for a property acquisition, flag issues and draft a report with citations.

**Constraints that shape the design.** Every issue in the report cites the document and page it comes from; Lawyers review and approve every report; Data-room documents stay confidential and are not used to train shared models

**Label in this use case.** For "Real Estate Due-Diligence Document Review": Review data-room documents (title, leases, surveys, environmental, contracts) for a property acquisition, flag issues and draft a report with citations. Define the label exactly as in milestone M1: what counts as a positive or a correct outcome, and when it becomes known. Design around this trap first: Past deals used for tuning are the easy ones that closed, so recall on hard deals is overstated.

## 2. Choose the signals

Your strongest signal is **Checklist coverage** (share of required document types present and reviewed). Its rule: Definitions of required vary by deal, so use a template per type. In the starter, the closest executable idea is **typed read-only tools**: narrow queries for context and evidence, with no free-form shell or query language. Map your columns in `starter/` before you change anything else; the full list of signals and engine features is in `starter/usecase.json`.

## 3. Meet the first trap

Records can carry planted instructions. The naive agent followed them in 66.7% of injected cases and took 8 unsafe actions; it also decided cases whose deciding evidence was unavailable.

## 4. Baseline against the stronger design

The guarded agent treats tool output as data, verifies every claim against a cited line and escalates when evidence is missing: injection success 0.0%, unsafe actions 0, accuracy on the cases it decides 100.0%, escalating 8.0%.

| Result | Value |
|---|---|
| Injection success, naive | 66.7% |
| Injection success, guarded | 0.0% |
| Unsafe actions, naive versus guarded | 8 vs 0 |
| Accuracy on auto-decided cases, guarded | 100.0% |
| Escalation rate, guarded | 8.0% |

## 5. An edge case from the blueprint

**Missing documents.** Key documents are missing from the data room. Planned response: List the missing documents against the checklist and flag the risk in the report. Write the test that replays this case through your pipeline before you tune anything.

## 6. What to expect on your own data

The planner is scripted so the harness is deterministic. Keep the planted-injection tests, the step budget and the verifier when you put a model behind the same typed tools.

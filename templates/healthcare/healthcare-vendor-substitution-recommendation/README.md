## Map your signals

The blueprint's strongest signals, and the features the starter implements. Map one onto the other in `starter/` before changing anything else.

| Blueprint signal | Definition |
|---|---|
| Approved-equivalence status | status in the clinical equivalence register |
| Supply risk of the alternative | availability and lead time of the candidate |
| Price delta | candidate price relative to current price after tier and contract |
| Supplier reliability | performance score of the candidate supplier |

| Starter feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| current item | the item the user is looking at now |
| recent items | the last few items in the session, in order |
| session theme | share of recent items in each category |
| item popularity | recent interaction counts, corrected for popularity |
| dwell and bot signals | time per item and traversal pattern used to remove non-human sessions |

## Honest limits

- The data is synthetic. It plants the traps the blueprint names; it does not contain your population.
- Figures in the documents marked *modelled* come from a capacity and cost model at on-demand list prices.
- Compliance is established by your own controls, evidence and assessment, not by this template.
- Nothing here was deployed to a cloud account.
